About Botswana

A destination of remarkable landscapes, wildlife, cultural heritage and community stories.

Discover more

Nature, culture and open horizons.

Botswana is a landlocked country in Southern Africa. Its diverse environments include the Okavango Delta, the Kalahari region, salt pans, rivers and wildlife areas.

Travel offers opportunities to learn about the country's people, traditions and conservation efforts. Visitors can help protect destinations by following local guidance, respecting wildlife and cultural sites, and choosing responsible operators.

Travel thoughtfully

Responsible tourism matters

Small choices can help protect the places and communities that make a journey meaningful.

Respect nature

Keep a safe distance from wildlife, follow guides' instructions and leave natural places as you found them.

Respect culture

Ask before taking photographs of people and follow local customs and community guidance.

Support local

Where possible, use locally owned services and purchase locally made products responsibly.
